About this site

What is Taste of Japan?

A restaurant guide that introduces Japanese dining to overseas visitors. Japanese food has more variety — and more unwritten rules — than most travelers expect. This site explains restaurants, prices, etiquette and useful phrases in plain English.

What's inside

  • A directory of restaurants with prices, ratings and access info
  • A food guide for first-timers (sushi, ramen, tempura, kaiseki…)
  • Dining etiquette: the DOs and DON'Ts
  • A mini phrasebook and FAQ
