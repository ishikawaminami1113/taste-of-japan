Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-09

Taste of Japan ("this site") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information is collected and how it is used.

Information we collect

This site does not collect personal information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) through forms or analytics. Server access logs are kept by the hosting provider for technical purposes only.

Local storage (favorites)

The heart icon saves your favorite restaurants using your browser's local storage. This list stays on your own device — it is never sent to us or to any third party, and it is removed if you clear your browser data.

Photographs

The photographs on this site show Japanese food and dining scenes in general, not the restaurants listed. They are supplied by Pexels under the Pexels License.

Advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google uses cookies, including the DART cookie, to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet. Third-party ad vendors may collect non-personal data such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited.

How to opt out

  • Google ad settings: adssettings.google.com
  • Your browser's cookie settings (you can disable or delete cookies at any time)

Third-party links

This site contains links to external sites (e.g., Google Maps). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the most recent revision.
